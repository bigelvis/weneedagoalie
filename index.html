What Happens When I Sign Up?

We Need A Goalie is run by a hockey player, for hockey players.

Your information — name, email, rinks, and skill level — is only used to match you with teams looking for a goalie. We don't sell your data, share it with third parties, or use it for anything else.

We may occasionally send a site update if something significant changes. That's it.

You can unsubscribe at any time from any email we send. 🏒
